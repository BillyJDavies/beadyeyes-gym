Training Tips

Progressive Overload

Each week, do one thing — add 1 rep, or increase weight. Do not chase both. Small consistent gains add up dramatically over months.

Deload Week (Every 4 Weeks)

Drop weight by 10% and slow every rep to 3 seconds on the lowering phase. This prevents plateau and lets joints recover. You often come back stronger.

Superset Tempo

Within pairs: 30-45 sec rest. Between pairs: 75-90 sec rest. Keep a phone timer running. This is what keeps sessions under 45 minutes.

Mind-Muscle Connection

On isolation exercises — laterals, curls, flys — feel the muscle working. Lighter weight with real contraction beats heavy sloppy reps every time.

Deadlift — King of Exercises

Lower reps are fine here (5-8). Focus on hip hinge not back bend. Push the floor away, do not pull the bar up. Braced core throughout every rep.

Nutrition Timing

Training at 6am? Have 20-30g protein within 30 minutes of finishing. Even a shake counts. Skipping post-workout nutrition limits muscle protein synthesis significantly.

The Honest Rule

3 solid sessions beats 4 rushed ones. If short on time, cut the burnout, not the superset pairs. Protect the main work.
